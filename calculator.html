Claims columns

  • course_code, unit_code, postcode: required
  • suburb: needed only where a postcode spans different loadings
  • student_id: completion payment is paid once per student + course
  • result_code: 51/52 = RPL; hours overrides the unit's payment hours
  • concession, fee_exempt, qualification_issued: Y/N
  • std_fee_per_hour: the RTO's fee; defaults to the AQF reduction
  • volume: multiplier for forecasts (default 1); aqf_level overrides the derived level

How the subsidy is calculated

per hour = max(0, base rate[FOE] × indexation × (1 + location loading) − AQF reduction)
subsidy = per hour × course adjustment % × payment hours × RPL factor × volume
plus the completion payment once per student and course when the qualification is issued, plus concession reimbursement min(max(fee − min fee, 0), max reimb.) × hours.

Assumptions (flagged where the Framework is silent)

  • Indexation is applied as one compounded factor with no rounding between years.
  • Fee-exempt students: the AQF reduction (assumed student fee) is not deducted, so government pays it.
  • Money is rounded to the cent per row (each unit claim), so totals equal the sum of exported rows.
  • Negative per-hour results are clamped to $0 and flagged on the row.
  • AQF level is derived from the course name; unrecognised courses are flagged and need an aqf_level, never guessed.
  • Concession: the full reimbursement is passed on to the student (Framework §11), so the student pays the standard fee minus the reimbursement.
  • Rates are those at the time fetch.py last ran. Re-run it to pick up new Framework versions.
